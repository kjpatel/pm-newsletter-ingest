## Summary

This edition of Community Wisdom curates the most valuable discussions from Lenny's subscriber Slack community, covering practical challenges including building without clear PM requirements, pre-selling products before development, pricing strategies for fast-moving B2B SaaS, and career guidance from a year-long job search.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build before defining requirements**: Engage with actual users and iterate based on real feedback rather than waiting for perfect PM specifications upfront.
- **Validate market demand first**: Pre-sell or get commitments from customers before investing engineering resources into full product development.
- **Establish dynamic pricing frameworks**: Create pricing models that can adapt quickly to market conditions rather than rigid structures for B2B SaaS products.
- **Learn from extended job searches**: Document lessons from prolonged career transitions to help others navigate similar situations and build resilience.
