# 🎙️ How I AI: How this PM uses Claude to handle 70% to 80% of his workday

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-i-ai-how-this-pm-uses-claude)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky | **Date**: Aug 31, 2026

---

## Summary

Daniel Blum, a PM at Melio, built a self-improving Claude-powered system that handles 70-80% of his workday by combining Cowork, personalized context files, and feedback loops that learn from his edits. The system demonstrates that effective AI automation depends on architecture and ongoing context management rather than the tool itself.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build architecture around tool integration**: Design your AI system to update its own core files and connect to tools you already use (Notion, Slack, email) rather than choosing based on the platform alone—this enables continuous improvement over time.
- **Create persistent context through recurring updates**: Establish a system that refreshes context files every few weeks with voice memos, links, and updates to keep Claude's knowledge current, and have it flag unfamiliar terms it encounters so you can fill knowledge gaps efficiently.
- **Implement feedback telemetry loops**: Capture the difference between Claude's drafts and your final versions weekly, then use these signals to automatically recommend system improvements—treating your personal AI workflow like a product with analytics.
- **Expect slow payoff in early weeks but push through**: The first few weeks will feel frustrating as the system learns, but once centralized information builds up, you can accomplish a week's worth of work in a single focused day.
- **Design onboarding for team adoption**: Create a 15-minute personalized onboarding flow that connects employees' tools, maps their colleagues, and learns their voice so other team members can benefit from your system foundation without starting from scratch.
