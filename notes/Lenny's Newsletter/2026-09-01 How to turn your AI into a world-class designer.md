# How to turn your AI into a world-class designer

**Source**: [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-turn-your-ai-into-a-world)
**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky (featuring Anshu Chimala) | **Date**: Sep 01, 2026

---

## Summary

AI models are naturally poor designers because they predict the most likely token at each step, but by using specific techniques to push them beyond their default patterns, you can unlock their creative potential and generate distinctive, high-quality designs.

## Key Takeaways

- **Use seed strings for variety**: Generate random alphanumeric strings and use them as design inspiration to break AI models out of their tendency to produce the same predictable outputs repeatedly.
- **Be ambitious and specific with prompts**: Instead of generic requests, provide detailed creative direction with specific inspirations (video games, art movements, interior design trends) to guide the model toward distinctive design choices.
- **Apply the Discover-Define-Deliver framework**: Structure your AI design process like the Double Diamond method—first explore broad possibilities, then define a unique identity by chaining models together, then polish the final result.
- **Chain multiple models together**: Combine different AI models (e.g., Claude Opus 5 + GPT-5.6 Sol) in sequence to fully realize your design's potential and access each model's unique strengths.
- **Focus on feeling and emotional response**: Great design creates memorable, unexpected experiences—guide AI to make bold, rule-bending choices rather than safe, committee-approved decisions by emphasizing the emotional impact you want users to feel.
