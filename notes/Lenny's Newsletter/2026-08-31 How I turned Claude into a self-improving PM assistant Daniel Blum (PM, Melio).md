# How I turned Claude into a self-improving PM assistant | Daniel Blum (PM, Melio)

**Author**: Lenny Rachitsky (featuring Daniel Blum) | **Date**: Aug 31, 2026

## Summary

Daniel Blum, a PM at Melio, shares how he built a self-improving Claude and Cowork-based system that automates 70-80% of his workflow—including weekly prep, morning briefs, and continuous skill development—and how he scaled this personal infrastructure across his entire team.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build two foundational rules first**: Focus on systematic thinking and structured context management rather than jumping between tools; these principles make any AI system genuinely powerful.
- **Automate recurring PM overhead**: Set up weekly prep automation (like filling a Notion board on Sundays) and morning briefs that teach Claude internal jargon automatically, freeing mental energy for higher-value work.
- **Create self-improvement feedback loops**: Use an 'Improve' skill that watches your editing patterns, spots recurring friction points, and suggests new capabilities to build—turning your actual work into a signal for what to optimize next.
- **Scale personal systems through thoughtful UX**: Package your AI setup into onboarding plugins (like Melio's 15-minute Workstation) rather than assuming others will replicate your workflow independently.
- **Identify and plan for capability gaps**: Recognize the 20% of work Claude still can't do autonomously (like complex judgment calls) and use reclaimed time to focus on those high-leverage activities instead.
