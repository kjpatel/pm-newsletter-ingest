## Summary

As AI handles more execution work, product leaders must shift from 'rowing' (doing) to 'steering' (directing), making human judgment, ambition, and vision the new differentiators in the age of persistent AI coworkers.

## Key Takeaways

- **Shift your role from execution to direction**: As AI takes on more execution tasks, PMs should focus on steering direction, raising team ambition, and making high-judgment calls rather than doing the work themselves.
- **Elevate ambition across your organization**: Ambition has become the new bottleneck for companies—help your team members think bigger and pursue more meaningful goals, as this is now the highest-leverage PM activity.
- **Build for capabilities 2-3 months ahead**: Plan and design products for AI model capabilities that will exist in the near future, not just current capabilities, to stay ahead of the curve and maximize product velocity.
- **Use writing as thinking, not just reporting**: Encourage your team to write to clarify their own thinking rather than merely documenting decisions, which leads to better problem-solving and strategic clarity.
- **Cultivate judgment and taste as your moat**: In an AI-saturated world, your ability to make nuanced decisions, discern quality, and inspire others becomes your unfair advantage over both competitors and AI systems.
