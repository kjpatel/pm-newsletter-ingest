## Summary

Anthropic releases Fable 5.1 with removal of its controversial data retention policy and enhanced caching capabilities that benefit both users and the company's infrastructure efficiency.

## Key Takeaways

- **Reverse unpopular policies** when they conflict with user needs—Anthropic's complete removal of the Fable data retention policy shows that listening to customer feedback can strengthen product-market fit.
- **Use technical improvements as policy fixes** - Increased caching serves dual purposes of improving performance and enabling policy changes, creating win-win scenarios for both users and infrastructure.
- **Enterprise safeguards matter** - Focus on frontier model safety features that give enterprise customers confidence in deployment, especially as AI capabilities advance.
