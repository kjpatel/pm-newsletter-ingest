# An Interview with OpenAI President Greg Brockman About Astra and Alignment

**Source**: [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-openai-president-greg-brockman-about-astra-and-alignment/)
**Author**: Ben Thompson | **Date**: Sep 04, 2026

---

## Summary

Greg Brockman, OpenAI's President and co-founder, discusses his path from Stripe CTO to leading OpenAI, the development of Astra, and OpenAI's approach to alignment and security in the context of competitive pressures from Microsoft and NVIDIA.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build great teams deliberately**: Brockman emphasizes that technical skill alone is insufficient; creating environments and cultures that enable teams to deliver exceptional results is central to leadership and innovation.
- **Balance consumer visibility with strategic positioning**: OpenAI must navigate being both a consumer-facing company (through ChatGPT's billion users) and a supplier to enterprises, requiring careful alignment strategy to maintain independence.
- **Alignment requires proactive security measures**: OpenAI's commitment to alignment means treating cybersecurity seriously from the ground up, not as an afterthought, especially given the company's critical position in the AI value chain.
- **Excellence at scale demands finding talent where others overlook**: Brockman's North Dakota background and trajectory shows that exceptional people can emerge from non-obvious locations if given access to competitive environments and national-level opportunities.
