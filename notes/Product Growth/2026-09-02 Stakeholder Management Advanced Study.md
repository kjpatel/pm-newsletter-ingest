# Stakeholder Management: Advanced Study

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/stakeholder-management-for-product-managers)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta | **Date**: Sep 02, 2026

---

## Summary

Stakeholder management is a critical PM skill that AI won't replace, requiring intentional relationship-building through trust-building, understanding stakeholder needs, and strategic communication.

## Key Takeaways

- **Map your stakeholders systematically** - Create a stakeholder map organized by proximity (reporting line, core team, leaders, influencers) rather than relying on org charts to understand who truly influences your work.
- **Build trust progressively through a pyramid** - Start with Level 1 (avoiding mistakes and executing reliably), move to Level 2 (doing good work visible to each stakeholder), then advance to Levels 3-4 (treating stakeholders as customers with discovery and negotiation skills).
- **Close visibility gaps strategically** - Audit what exposure each stakeholder has to your work versus what they value, then deliberately fill gaps (e.g., sharing customer insights via Slack bots, presenting at all-hands) to show impact aligned with their interests.
- **Use discovery and tactical empathy on stakeholders** - Apply the same customer research skills to understand stakeholder motivations, priorities, and concerns through thoughtful questions and active listening rather than assumptions.
- **Practice influence without authority** - Learn negotiation fundamentals from resources like 'Never Split the Difference' and 'Influence Without Authority' to create buy-in for ideas and navigate organizational dynamics effectively.
