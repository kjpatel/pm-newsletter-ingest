# How to Gain AI Product Management Experience Without Being an AI PM

**Source**: [Product Growth](https://www.news.aakashg.com/p/synthetic-ai-pm-experience)
**Author**: Aakash Gupta (featuring Nancy Li) | **Date**: Aug 27, 2026

---

## Summary

With AI PM jobs now representing 46% of open PM roles but requiring prior AI experience, candidates face a catch-22. This guide shows how to build 'synthetic experience'—real AI products shipped independently—to demonstrate AI PM capabilities and break into the market without prior AI PM titles.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build synthetic AI products** to prove you can ship features at scale; construct real projects you can discuss authentically in interviews, not fabricated experience that collapses under technical scrutiny.
- **Document your product decisions** throughout your synthetic project so you can articulate eval strategies, user feedback loops, and launch decisions—the same questions hiring managers will ask.
- **Update your LinkedIn headline and portfolio** with your synthetic work; 69% of AI PM listings require prior AI/ML experience, making visible proof of shipping critical in a hiring manager's market.
- **Target the 1-2 year gap** in AI experience that most listings actually require; most positions want 5+ years of PM tenure plus just 1-2 years of AI-specific shipping experience, making synthetic work highly achievable.
- **Showcase through multiple channels** (resume, LinkedIn posts, portfolio projects) to build credibility; real case studies show candidates landing AI PM roles at major companies like Cisco after demonstrating synthetic experience.
