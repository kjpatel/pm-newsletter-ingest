## Summary

Tyler Folkman, Chief AI and Product Officer at JobNimbus, explains how to build effective product loops in Claude Code by designing self-contained systems that fetch inputs, execute work, apply gates, and iterate—moving beyond simple prompting to true agentic engineering.

## Key Takeaways

- **Write your first loop by hand** before automating it with AI. Manually authoring skills ensures you capture nuanced requirements and develop deeper understanding of what the loop should accomplish, rather than becoming dependent on AI-generated solutions.
- **Design loops with a gate mechanism** that includes fetching inputs, executing work, applying decision gates, writing named artifacts, and automatically re-fetching. This self-contained cycle is what separates legitimate loops from simple prompts or one-off agent runs.
- **Build these 5 essential PM loops**: customer outreach (time-saving discovery), project status (identifying blockers), thinking partner (pushing team cognition rather than doing work), divergent prototypes (testing multiple variants), and quality watchdog (preventing bug multiplication from faster shipping).
- **Avoid outsourcing your thinking** through AI-generated analysis. Use loops to push back on decisions and validate assumptions with customer data and industry benchmarks, not to replace your judgment—this protects your credibility as a PM in 2026.
- **Prioritize quality assurance loops** with engineering. If you ship twice as fast with AI but maintain the same quality rate, customers experience twice as many bugs—making automated quality monitoring critical for sustainable velocity.
