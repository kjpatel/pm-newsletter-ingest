# The Price of Entry to the Frontier

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/the-great-segmentation/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 31, 2026

---

## Summary

Frontier AI access is becoming increasingly restricted and gatekept, with labs establishing exclusive partnerships, governments imposing export controls, and even open-source models adding commercial revenue thresholds. Access rather than price is now the primary scarcity in frontier AI.

## Key Takeaways

- **Expect hardcoded defaults**: Enterprises should anticipate that major platforms will bundle specific AI models by default (like Salesforce with Claude), requiring negotiation for flexibility and data governance control.
- **Plan for vendor lock-in**: Model-agnostic architectures are becoming obsolete; evaluate long-term switching costs and negotiate explicit contract terms allowing model provider changes before committing to integrated solutions.
- **Prepare for tiered open-source**: Open-source models now impose commercial licensing thresholds when revenue exceeds certain levels (e.g., $10B); factor these conversion costs into infrastructure planning for scaled deployments.
- **Account for sovereignty constraints**: Government export restrictions and geolocation requirements are fragmenting access to frontier models; map your data residency requirements and regulatory obligations before selecting vendors.
- **Invest in open alternatives**: Nvidia's $26B+ commitment to open-weight ecosystems creates a structural hedge; diversify toward open-source foundations to avoid complete reliance on proprietary lab restrictions.
