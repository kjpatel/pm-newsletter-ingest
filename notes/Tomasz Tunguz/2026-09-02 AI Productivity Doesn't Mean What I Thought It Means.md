# AI Productivity Doesn't Mean What I Thought It Means

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/ai-productivity-doesnt-mean-what-i-thought/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Sep 02, 2026

---

## Summary

AI productivity in writing doesn't reduce effort—it raises the quality ceiling by automating structural work, allowing writers to concentrate effort on rhetorical precision rather than drafting mechanics. The author's data shows that while line-level editing time remains constant, the quality floor has risen significantly, preventing poor drafts from reaching readers.

## Key Takeaways

- **Reframe productivity metrics**: AI doesn't shrink human effort in knowledge work; it shifts effort from mechanical tasks to higher-level craft. Track quality floor improvement alongside time savings.
- **Build feedback loops**: Implement closed-loop systems with AI agents that learn your style over time. The author's nightly style guideline updates created a personalized editing standard that improved consistency.
- **Focus on preventing bad output**: AI's real value in writing is eliminating low-quality drafts before publication. The 10th percentile quality score nearly doubled (2.59→3.81) while top-tier writing improved more modestly.
