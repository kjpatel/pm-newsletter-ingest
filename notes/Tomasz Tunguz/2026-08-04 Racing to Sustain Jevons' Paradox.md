# Racing to Sustain Jevons' Paradox

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/what-if-gpu-prices-double/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 04, 2026

---

## Summary

Despite rising AI capacity constraints across hyperscalers, model makers are sustaining Jevons' Paradox through strategic pricing segmentation across premium, mid-market, and value tiers, allowing increased total consumption even as premium prices rise.

## Key Takeaways

- **Segment your AI pricing strategy** across premium ($50/M tokens), mid-market (40% of premium cost), and value ($1-5% of premium) tiers to capture price-sensitive workloads and maintain total consumption growth.
- **Invest in router infrastructure** to dynamically route queries to the optimal model tier, as routers become the strategic battleground between labs and the plumbing that sustains Jevons' Paradox.
- **Defend market position with tier coverage** - major labs must offer competitive entries in all three tiers or lose router auctions to competitors, while startups can win by owning a single high-value frontier point.
- **Monitor the mid-market emergence** as the new battleground, where models like GPT-5.6 Sol and Kimi K3 deliver 96% of frontier intelligence at 40% of cost, reshaping where competition intensifies.
