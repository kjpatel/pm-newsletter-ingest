# A Winner in Every Category

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/a-winner-in-every-category/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 11, 2026

---

## Summary

Despite the SaaS market downturn, each major category has one dominant leader trading at massive premiums by positioning around AI infrastructure—from CrowdStrike's threat detection for AI endpoints to Shopify's agent-driven commerce—proving the market rewards companies with durable AI-native business models.

## Key Takeaways

- **Identify the AI harness in your category**: Leaders like CrowdStrike (3.9x peers), Cloudflare (3.4x), and Shopify (8.1x) command premiums by owning the critical infrastructure layer that AI agents depend on—map where agents will need your service to survive.
- **Build defensible moats through non-Internet training data**: Samsara trades at 10.8x its vertical median because its 25 trillion annual data points and dash-cam imagery are irreplaceable training assets—focus on proprietary data streams competitors cannot acquire.
- **Revenue will follow agent deployment**: Cloudflare, Twilio, and Salesforce all see disproportionate growth from agent-driven demand (non-human traffic, voice agents, Agentforce reaching $1.2B ARR)—embed your product into the agent workflow, not the human one.
- **Multiples reward durability over growth**: CrowdStrike grows slower than Rubrik (23% vs 46%) yet trades at 3.3x higher multiples—the market values AI-enabled revenue recurrence and customer lock-in more than pure growth velocity.
- **Vertical focus creates defensible pricing**: ServiceNow (7.4x vs 3.4x median), Samsara, and Figma demonstrate that specialization in AI governance, data, and design creates pricing power—go deep in one category rather than broad.
