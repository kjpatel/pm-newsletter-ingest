# The Ads Model for Prompts Vertically Integrates AI

**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Sep 03, 2026

## Summary

Meta's new two-tier pricing for Muse Spark—charging 92% less for data-sharing versus private inference—reveals a $1.24 per million token value for user data and enables Meta to vertically integrate AI's data supply chain, mirroring the advertising model by subsidizing access in exchange for training data.

## Key Takeaways

- **Recognize the data arbitrage:** Meta's pricing explicitly quantifies user data value at $1.24/m tokens; enterprises paying for privacy are essentially funding Meta's post-training data acquisition at a fraction of specialized vendor costs.
- **Understand the business model shift:** Foundation models are no longer pure infrastructure utilities—compute is now a currency being traded directly for training tokens needed to build frontier models, following the proven ads model playbook.
- **Prepare for vertical integration:** Similar to how search and social platforms captured behavioral data directly from users, AI providers will increasingly bypass third-party labeling vendors by turning inference networks into self-funding data flywheels.
- **Calculate your data subsidy:** For enterprises processing 1B tokens daily, the privacy premium amounts to a $454K annual surcharge; understanding this spread helps assess whether data retention policies are worth the cost differential.
