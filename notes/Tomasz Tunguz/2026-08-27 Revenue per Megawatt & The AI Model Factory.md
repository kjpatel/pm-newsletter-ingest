# Revenue per Megawatt & The AI Model Factory

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/revenue-per-megawatt/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 27, 2026

---

## Summary

AI model companies generate profit by buying wholesale electricity and reselling it as cognitive work, with revenue-per-megawatt economics enabling them to fund continuous model improvement cycles. The shift from negative to positive gross margins demonstrates that inference profits can fuel the next generation of model training.

## Key Takeaways

- **Monitor revenue-per-megawatt metrics** as the key indicator of AI model company profitability—Anthropic achieved $50M per megawatt in 2026 against $10-15M costs, enabling reinvestment in training.
- **Efficiency compounds returns** more than raw intelligence—models like GLM-5.3-Flash deliver identical performance at 90-97% lower cost through parameter optimization, generating more tokens per megawatt for greater margins.
- **Build model factories, not artisanal studios** by treating model development as an industrial process with thousands of experiments rather than hand-tuned iterations, as Poolside demonstrated by shipping Laguna S 2.1 in 52 days.
- **Reinvest inference margins strategically** into training to create a compounding asset cycle—the 40-50% gross margin Anthropic achieved in 2025-2026 funds the cost reduction and efficiency gains needed to stay competitive.
