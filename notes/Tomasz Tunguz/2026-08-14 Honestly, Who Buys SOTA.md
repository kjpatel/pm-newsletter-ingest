# Honestly, Who Buys SOTA?

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/model-release-exhaustion/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 14, 2026

---

## Summary

Despite state-of-the-art AI models improving rapidly, 84% of tokens on OpenRouter are generated by non-frontier models that deliver 77% of frontier performance at 2.5% of the cost, suggesting buyers optimize for price over performance and that frontier model share will plateau as 'good enough' models become sufficient.

## Key Takeaways

- **Monitor price elasticity trends**: Track how frontier models capture share—Fable 5 generated 75% of GPT-5.6 Sol's revenue despite higher costs, indicating price sensitivity will intensify as models converge in capability.
- **Plan for model consolidation**: Enterprise customers will concentrate spend on one or two vendors similar to the cloud era, so build vendor flexibility and multi-model strategies into your product roadmap.
- **Right-size your model selection**: Evaluate whether your use case truly needs frontier models—77% of frontier performance at 2.5% of the cost is often sufficient, especially for application deployment versus software engineering.
- **Expect declining SOTA market share**: Each new state-of-the-art release will capture less incremental share than predecessors as the performance gap closes from below, making nine-figure training investments harder to justify.
