# AI Harness' ARR Multiples

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/ai-harness-arr-multiples/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 09, 2026

---

## Summary

AI harness companies crossing $100M ARR are commanding valuations between 25-125x revenue, with premiums determined by market category position rather than growth rates, representing a return to 2021's 100x multiples but on 3x faster underlying growth.

## Key Takeaways

- **Track category leadership** - Companies like Harvey, Legora, and Sierra trade within narrow valuation bands (50-100x ARR) based on their market position, not growth speed, suggesting investors price market dominance over velocity.
- **Monitor the 100x premium's return** - The 100x ARR multiple last seen in 2021 has reemerged in 2026, but on significantly faster growth fundamentals (3x faster), indicating the market is repricing AI infrastructure companies with higher conviction.
- **Expect continued multiple expansion** - Recent evidence shows multiples accelerating further into 2026 for companies like Legora, Sierra, and Ramp, particularly in favorable fundraising windows, suggesting AI harness valuations may continue rising despite scale.
