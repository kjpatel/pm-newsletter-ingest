# The AI Bullwhip

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/the-ai-cost-stack/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 23, 2026

---

## Summary

AI infrastructure faces a cascading 'Bullwhip Effect' where hardware bottlenecks sequentially migrate from GPUs to memory to CPUs to storage, with each constraint amplified by multi-year manufacturing lags and locking in higher baseline costs, particularly in expensive data center construction now exceeding $20 billion per gigawatt.

## Key Takeaways

- **Monitor the supply chain cascade**: Track how relieving pressure at one infrastructure bottleneck predictably shifts scarcity to the next component with a 1-3 year delay, affecting your hardware procurement strategy and timeline.
- **Plan for physical infrastructure costs**: Data center construction now represents 50% of facility costs at $20b/GW with 3-year lead times on transformers and turbines; budget accordingly for capital-intensive deployment plans through 2028-2031.
- **Recognize the demand-supply mismatch risk**: Over $2B in transformer expansions and NAND fabs arriving in 2027-2028 could create overcapacity if software revenue growth doesn't match infrastructure capex—position your AI applications to maximize utilization.
- **Prepare for inventory shocks**: Each wave of hardware scarcity (SSDs up 80%, DRAM in 60s %, CPUs up 27%) forces downstream architectural changes; design systems flexible enough to adapt when preferred components become unavailable or unaffordically expensive.
