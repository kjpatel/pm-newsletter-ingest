# AI is a Terrible Ghostwriter

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/ai-ghost-writing/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 03, 2026

---

## Summary

AI excels as an editor but fails as a ghostwriter because its generic voice lacks individual authenticity; in an age of AI slop, readers increasingly detect when AI has written content, making it essential to preserve authorial voice through distinctive writing techniques.

## Key Takeaways

- **Use AI as an editor, not a ghostwriter** — leverage AI's rigor for statistical analysis, structure feedback (deks and nut grafs), and critique, but don't let it homogenize your voice.
- **Develop distinctive writing signatures** — employ unique punctuation (ampersands), neologisms, grammatical flourishes, and sentence structures that readers will recognize as authentically yours.
- **Understand the authenticity test** — post-AI readers actively test for AI-written content; preserve musicality and individuality in your writing to maintain reader trust and differentiate from generic AI slop.
