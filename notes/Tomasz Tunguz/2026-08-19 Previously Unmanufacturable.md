# Previously Unmanufacturable

**Source**: [Tomasz Tunguz](https://tomtunguz.com/previously-unmanufacturable/)
**Author**: Tomasz Tunguz | **Date**: Aug 19, 2026

---

## Summary

AI enables non-experts to operate complex software by translating natural English intent into application-specific grammar, as demonstrated by a non-technical founder using AI to design a previously unmanufacturable dress through CAD software. While this democratizes tool access, expertise remains essential for solving deep system problems.

## Key Takeaways

- **Make English your interface** - Design AI-powered products where users describe outcomes in natural language while agents handle the application's technical grammar, reducing onboarding friction for product-led growth companies.
- **Shift from screen design to harness design** - Move product management focus from designing individual screens to building safe, complete harnesses that let agents operate systems without manual guidance.
- **Invest in documentation as product design** - Prioritize well-documented, complex software architecture that agents can understand and reason over, as documentation becomes essential UX rather than ancillary support.
- **Preserve depth expertise** - Recognize that while AI handles routine operations, expert systems architects remain critical for solving hard problems and understanding how pieces fit together.
