## Summary

Clay's publicly shared AI writing policy provides a framework for responsible AI use in startup communications, emphasizing accountability, thoughtful composition, and quality over quantity through four guiding principles.

## Key Takeaways

- **Establish clear AI guidelines** - Document your startup's specific policies on AI tool usage to ensure consistency and accountability across teams.
- **Prioritize authorship responsibility** - Require that team members stand behind every idea and sentence they publish, maintaining human accountability even when using AI assistance.
- **Recognize writing as strategic thinking** - Allocate more time to the writing process itself than to consuming the output, treating composition as a core business activity.
- **Quality over efficiency** - Push back on the assumption that AI saves time; instead use it to improve clarity and depth rather than just produce more content faster.
